Your Retirement Estimates

Test retirement choices quickly, see what changes the outcome, and adjust your plan while you still have time.

About This Retirement Planner

What it does: This planner lets you quickly test whether your projected retirement income, savings, spending, healthcare costs, taxes, and investment assumptions may support the retirement you are considering. Instead of assuming one smooth investment return, it runs thousands of Monte Carlo simulations using different sequences of market returns so you can see a range of possible outcomes.

Purpose: The goal is not to predict your future or tell you what financial decision to make. It is to help you explore possibilities early enough to change your plan if needed. You can test questions such as: What if I retire two years later? What if I spend less? What if healthcare costs are higher? What if inflation remains elevated? What if markets are weak during my first retirement years? By changing one assumption at a time and rerunning the simulation, you can identify which choices have the greatest effect on your retirement outlook.

How to use the planner:
  1. Enter your best current estimates in the sections below. Use the blue information icons for guidance on each field.
  2. Enter retirement spending excluding healthcare, because healthcare is modeled separately.
  3. Choose realistic investment return, volatility, inflation, tax, Social Security, pension, and healthcare assumptions.
  4. Leave the stress tests off for your baseline run, then use them to test difficult conditions such as poor early returns, higher inflation, reduced Social Security, higher healthcare costs, or a longer life.
  5. Choose the number of Monte Carlo simulations and click Run Monte Carlo Simulation.
  6. Review the Success Rate, portfolio withdrawal requirement, retirement balance, healthcare cost, charts, and year-by-year details.
  7. Change one or two assumptions and rerun. Compare how retirement age, spending, savings, guaranteed income, healthcare, or investment assumptions affect the result.
  8. Use the PDF report generators in the Results Dashboard to save or share the assumptions and results you want to discuss further.

Important: This is an educational planning tool, not financial, investment, tax, legal, or medical advice. The value of the planner is in comparing scenarios and understanding trade-offs, not in treating any single simulation result as a forecast or guarantee.
